Service 01 · Centralized Receiving & Distribution

One central facility. Zero curb chaos.

Can you imagine it the other way? Hundreds of trucks delivering food to individual restaurants — plus retail and parcel carriers — all needing clearance, all clogging the behind-the-scenes roads of the airport.

ALM provides, manages, and maintains the secured cross-docking facility where it all lands first. Consumables are processed and delivered to the correct tenant every day — 41 food & beverage sites and 9 retail locations at Dulles alone.

Service 02 · Tracking for Deliveries

Where's my product? Never a question.

We electronically track all food and retail merchandise inbound and outbound, so we know where your products are at all times — and so do you.

We work closely with concessionaire vendors to establish dependable, flexible arrival schedules, and we generate delivery schedules that plan staffing without wasting resources. Expedited delivery for emergency receipt and ready response to the unexpected is always available.

Service 03 · Escort for Subcontractors

Secure areas, without the staffing headache.

When subcontractors need access to secure areas — especially during construction — an escort must maintain physical and visual control of every escorted individual, the whole time.

ALM already has a large, vetted crew badged for sterile and secure areas. Leave the escorting to the third party that's already inside the fence — your project keeps moving and security stays whole.

Service 04 · Equipment Installation, Maintenance & Disposal

Your equipment, handled inside the fence.

Outside an airport, every business handles its own equipment. Inside one, those functions must be entrusted to a third party — and every vendor's list of equipment and maintenance needs is different.

ALM coordinates and supports every install, update, and disposal, with complete ongoing support — whether the business is a boutique, flower shop, gift shop, or restaurant.

Common Questions

Asked at every kickoff meeting.

Do my suppliers need airport badges or clearance?
No — that's the point. Suppliers and parcel carriers deliver to ALM's secure facility away from the terminal, like any normal delivery. ALM's badged, vetted crew carries everything from there through security to your door.
How do I know where my product is?
Every piece is electronically tracked inbound and outbound. From the moment your supplier's truck is unloaded to the moment your team signs at the concourse, the product's location is logged — and we can tell you at any time.
What does it cost?
Less than doing it yourself — by design. Consolidation removes redundant trucking, staffing, and waiting time; airports that centralize with ALM can count on at least a 60% reduction in logistics cost. Call 571-236-6294 for a quote scoped to your operation.
Can you handle refrigerated and frozen product?
Yes. The commissary operation is built around food & beverage first — cold chain is maintained from receiving through delivery, and all managers and supervisors are ServSafe certified before they handle any product.
What about emergencies and odd schedules?
Schedules are set with each vendor to be dependable and flexible — and expedited delivery for emergency receipts and unexpected situations is always available. We also flex with tenant, airport, TSA, and FAA requirements as they change.
We're opening a new location — can you help with the build-out?
Yes. Between escort services for your contractors, equipment installation and disposal, and delivery coordination during construction, ALM has supported openings from single kiosks to four restaurants in three months.
